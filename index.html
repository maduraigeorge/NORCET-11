How the estimate works

  • Aggregate view. Qualified candidates are processed in rank order. Within each gender, open (UR) seats go first to the best-ranked candidates, then each reserved quota fills from its own category. The last rank admitted in each pool is the cut-off shown.
  • Verdict. Safe means the rank is at least 10% inside the cut-off when only half of the unsplit seats are female. Likely means inside that cut-off by less than 10%. Borderline means inside only when all unsplit seats are female. Outside means beyond every case.
  • Institute view. Adds institute choice. Preferences of other candidates are unknown, so they are modelled. Each other candidate lists only the first N institutes of their order. The answer changes with the model and with N, so compare several settings before relying on it.
  • PwBD. Seats for persons with benchmark disability sit inside each quota total and are reserved horizontally. They are left out of the open and category pools here, and PwBD candidates compete only for open and category seats.

Not modelled

  • Eligibility checks at institutes (age, experience, registration, valid OBC-NCL or EWS certificates, NOC).
  • Candidates who do not fill choices or later decline a seat. The notice says there is one round and no waiting list.
  • Seat changes after 30 Oct 2026. Rishikesh is treated as an addition to its earlier seats, Bhopal and Mangalagiri as replaced by Addendum 38.
